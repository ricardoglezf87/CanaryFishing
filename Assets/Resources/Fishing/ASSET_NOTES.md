# Fishing visual assets

The OBJ models in `Models/` are original project assets generated for CanaryFishing:

- `FishingRodModel.obj`: tapered fishing rod with reel spool.
- `FishModel.obj`: fish body and tail mesh.
- `FloatModel.obj`: lathed fishing float.
- `BeachTerrain.obj`: UV-mapped beach terrain.
- `OceanSurface.obj`: UV-mapped subdivided ocean surface.

The textures in `Textures/` were generated with OpenAI ImageGen for this project:

- `CanarySand_Albedo.png`: seamless warm sand with volcanic mineral grains.
- `AtlanticWater_Albedo.png`: seamless Atlantic-blue water surface.

Both textures are albedo maps and are configured to repeat in Unity.
